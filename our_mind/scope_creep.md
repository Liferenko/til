# Scope creep

scope creep - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scope_creep
is continuous or uncontrolled growth in a project's scope

Common cause:
- Poorly defined project scope
- Project vagueness or complexity
- Failure to capture all requirements
- Lack of project management practices
- Addition of unnecessary features
- Low cost of change
- Internal additions
- Client additions
- Communication gap between project stakeholders

Management techniques:
1. Having clear foundational documents
2. Questioning or refusing changes
